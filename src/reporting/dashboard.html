Measurement contract

Callgrind Ir counts executed user-space machine instructions in the optimized build. Hardware-retired instruction totals are a separate measurement when PMU access is available. Inclusive costs contain descendants and overlap; self costs partition the collected instruction stream. Inlined or optimized-away functions may lack a separately attributable instruction count; an empty cell means unavailable, not zero.

Per-function clock timings come from a separate instrumented build and include profiler overhead. They are not production timings. Native geometry stage times and throughput use the uninstrumented PALP build. Geometry workloads check every point count, dimension, vertex count, IP result and reflexivity label before a result is accepted. They omit dual and normal-form construction.

Series are separated by workload hash, compiler, architecture, CPU, flags, profiler version, timing harness, measurement protocol and worker configuration. Improvements and regressions are both retained. A working-tree result is labelled explicitly; a single baseline does not establish a trend.
